Manage Projects

A form instead of hand-editing JSON. This runs entirely in your browser — nothing is saved automatically.

  1. Fill out the form below (or click a project on the left to edit it) and click Save to list.
  2. When you're done adding/editing, click Download projects.json at the bottom.
  3. Move the downloaded file into the projects/ folder, replacing the old projects.json.
  4. Drop any new images/videos into assets/images/projects/<slug>/ (or assets/videos/...) referencing those paths in the form.
  5. Commit and push as usual.
